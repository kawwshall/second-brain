# priorities

[[Home]] · [[Protocalls]]

Clubs describe importance within a time period.

redclub — must finish within that period.
blueclub — next priority after red.
whiteclub — good to finish if possible.
goldclub — extra progress.

Today's Red means must finish today. This year's Red means must finish this year. A yearly Red does not automatically belong in today's list and does not lose its annual importance because it is not urgent today.

Daily lists live in daily notes; annual lists live in [[This year]]. Keep short lists and link project notes when useful.
