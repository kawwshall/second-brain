Suggestions, not a large protocol system.

## priorities
Use [[priorities]] separately for today and this year.

## when stuck
Write what you do not understand. Find one example or ask someone who knows. Then do the next small thing.

## laps
Before starting, write what you want to finish. During the lap, jot down anything useful. Afterwards, write what happened and where to start next. Mahdi defaults to 25 minutes and a 5-minute break.

## closing the day
What got done? What is waiting? Where do I start tomorrow?
