# A short-list second brain

Start from `Home.md`. Put active-project links there and keep just enough outside-work context in `Work context.md` to know where to resume.

## Clubs have a time horizon

- **Red:** must finish within this period.
- **Blue:** next priority.
- **White:** good to finish if possible.
- **Gold:** extra progress.

Today's Red is due today. A year's Red matters within the year even when it is not urgent today. Keep daily lists in daily pages and annual lists in the year note. Do not copy every annual goal into every day.

## A daily page

Use your own short wording. The template gives you club headings, `logs`, and `what got done / where to start next`. Mahdi adds only timestamped words you actually enter under `logs`.

Personal dates use a two-digit year and three-digit day of year: `26·308`. Obsidian uses `YY·DDDD` to produce that notation. Calendar/API dates still use whatever format those systems require.

## Import useful context, not transcripts

After meaningful project work, keep a few lines: what happened / what's next / source link. Distinguish prepared, submitted, accepted, planned, and unverified. A chat title does not prove that the task finished. Keep credentials out of notes intended for sharing.

Protocol ideas belong in `protocalls/Protocalls.md` unless a topic becomes substantial enough to need its own page. These are suggestions; the setup should stay light.
